# Dataset and Evaluation Card

The benchmark uses Home Credit Default Risk, LendingClub Loan Data, and UCI Default of Credit Card Clients. Data are obtained from their original providers and are not redistributed. Labels have dataset-specific meanings and should not be interpreted as a universal definition of default. The code removes known post-outcome LendingClub variables, calculates transformations from development data only, uses entity-preserving stratified folds where identifiers permit, and selects classification thresholds on inner validation data. Public datasets can contain historical, geographic, demographic, and institutional biases. Results must not be used to make automated lending decisions without jurisdiction-specific validation, fairness assessment, calibration, human review, and regulatory approval.
